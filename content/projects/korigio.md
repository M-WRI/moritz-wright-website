Open-source desktop software for independent repair shops — customers, devices, repairs, diagnosis, print, and Wi-Fi team sync on the counter PC. Free. Your data stays local.

## About

Korigio is built for the workshop counter: intake, diagnose, approve, repair, collect. Data lives under AppData on Windows — SQLite, photos, backups — so the shop keeps working when the internet does not. No account, no cloud, no subscription. Team sync stays on the local Wi-Fi. Product site: [korigio.com](https://www.korigio.com/). Contact: info@korigio.com.
